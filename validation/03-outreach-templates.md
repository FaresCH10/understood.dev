# 03 — Outreach Templates (Research)

**Phase 1 · Version 0.1 · 2026-11-13**
**Purpose:** book 10 research conversations, not demos. Every message must be specific, short, and easy to decline.
**Rules:** personalize the first line; one ask per message; no attachments; no calendar link in the first message; follow up once after 4–5 days; stop after two follow-ups.

---

## 1. LinkedIn connection request (primary)

> Hi {name} — I'm researching how UAE facilities teams brief crews that speak several languages (Hindi, Urdu, Malayalam, Arabic). Not selling anything. Would you be open to a 25-minute conversation about how instructions reach workers on your sites today?

**Shorter variant:**

> Hi {name} — researching multilingual crew briefings in UAE facilities. Would value 25 minutes of your experience — no pitch, just learning how it works today.

**Personalize with one detail:** their site type, a post they wrote, a mutual connection, or the company's client base. Do not mention "AI" in the request.

---

## 2. After they accept

> Thanks for connecting, {name}. The reason I reached out: most supervisors I speak to send a voice note to a WhatsApp group and assume it landed. Is that roughly how it works on your sites, or have you found something better?
>
> If it's useful, I'd love 25 minutes to understand your current flow — what gets sent, in which languages, and how you know workers understood. Happy to work around your shift times.

---

## 3. Warm introduction request

> Hi {name} — I'm speaking with UAE facilities and HSE managers about how multilingual crews receive instructions. You mentioned you know {contact} at {company}; would you be comfortable introducing us? I'd keep it to a 25-minute research conversation, no sales pitch.
>
> A two-line intro is plenty — I can send you a note to forward.

---

## 4. Community message (after participating)

> Hi everyone — I'm researching how FM and cleaning teams brief crews that speak 4–5 languages. If you run sites in the UAE and have 20 minutes, I'd value your experience: what gets lost between the supervisor's voice note and the worker's understanding? Not selling anything; I'll share a summary of what I learn with the group.

Do not post links. Do not DM everyone who reacts. One post per community, maximum.

## 6. WhatsApp scheduling (after they agree)

> Thanks {name}. Would {day} at {time} or {day} at {time} work? It's 25 minutes, no pitch. I'll send a calendar invite with a call link.

Use GST (UTC+4). Offer two windows, Sunday–Thursday, 08:30–10:00 or 14:00–16:00. Avoid prayer times and shift handovers.

## 10. Referral ask

> One more ask, {name}: who else in your network runs crews in three or more languages? A two-line introduction is enough — I'll send you a note you can forward. I'm trying to speak with six operations leads and two HSE leads before I draw any conclusions.

---

## 11. Demo / pilot follow-up (only after a research call or demo)

> Thanks for the time today, {name}. Recap: one voice note → each worker's language (text + audio) → one comprehension question → a record of who understood.
>
> The pilot is eight weeks, one site, up to 150 workers, $490, fully credited to the first invoice. Success is measurable: 70% response, 60% understood, three instructions a week. If it's useful, I'll send the one-page agreement; the next step is a 20-minute kickoff with {champion}.

---

## 12. No-show / reschedule

> Hi {name} — looks like today didn't work; no problem. Would {day/time} or {day/time} suit better? If the timing is wrong in general, tell me and I'll check back next quarter.

After two no-shows: one final message, then move to a 90-day follow-up list. Do not guilt-trip.

---

## 13. Arabic version (short; native review before sending at scale)

> مرحباً {name} — أجري بحثاً حول كيفية إيصال التعليمات اليومية إلى العمال في فرق تتحدث عدة لغات في الإمارات. لست أبيع شيئاً؛ أبحث عن ٢٥ دقيقة لفهم الطريقة الحالية. هل يناسبك ذلك؟

Use only if the contact prefers Arabic. Have a native speaker review before sending at volume.

---

## 14. Weekly outreach quota

| Activity | Volume | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| New personalized connection requests | 50/week | 10/day, Monday–Friday |
| Follow-ups to accepted connections | 10/day | 15 minutes; no automation |
| Warm intro requests | 5/week | Alumni, former colleagues, interviewees |
| Community engagement | 15 min/day | Answer questions; no links |
| Interviews booked | 5/week | Target 10 in 2.5 weeks |

**LinkedIn safety:** stay under the platform's invitation limits; do not use automation tools; personalize every message; accept that most will not reply. Quality of the first line beats volume.

---

## 15. What not to send

- "Revolutionary AI platform" — the buyer does not care.
- A PDF deck in the first message.
- A calendar link with no context.
- "Quick call?" with no reason.
- A group blast or copied-paste message with the wrong company name.
- A discount or a free pilot before the problem is discussed.
- Anything that implies safety certification or worker surveillance.
